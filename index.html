Why restore

Four reasons to fix foggy headlights

1

See more at night

Oxidized plastic scatters your beam before it reaches the road. A clear lens puts that light back on the pavement in front of you, on dark streets and in the rain.

2

Skip the $300 part

A new headlight assembly runs $300 or more per side, and a shop charges labor on top to install it. Restoration costs $75 a light during the sale and keeps the housing you already have.

3

Raise your resale value

Yellow headlights make a well-kept car look years older. Buyers and dealers see that first and lower their offer to match.

4

Do it once

Your lens left the factory with a UV coating that has since worn off. I put a new UV-resistant clear coat on, so the fix lasts for as long as you own the car.

How it works

Four steps, one visit

Mobile service on Saturdays and Sundays, 8 AM to 4 PM. I bring everything to your driveway or work parking lot, so your car stays where it is.

  1. Mask

    I tape off the paint and trim around each light.

  2. Sand

    I wet sand through the yellow layer down to clean plastic.

  3. Polish

    I polish out the sanding marks until you can see the bulb again.

  4. Seal

    I spray a UV clear coat to keep the sun off the bare lens.
